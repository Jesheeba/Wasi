Privacy Policy

1. What this covers

This policy describes how Wasi CRM ("we", "us") collects, uses, and stores information when you use our WhatsApp CRM platform, including information processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta's Cloud API) on your behalf as a Meta Tech Provider.

2. Information we collect

Account information (business name, email, password hash); WhatsApp Business Account identifiers (WABA ID, phone number ID) and an access token issued by Meta, stored encrypted; contacts, chats, and messages you manage through the platform; usage and billing data; and any support ticket content you submit to us.

3. How we use information

To operate the CRM (send/receive WhatsApp messages on your behalf via the Cloud API), to provision and maintain your WhatsApp Business Account connection, to process billing, to provide support, and to comply with Meta's platform requirements as a Tech Provider.

4. Data sharing

We share data with Meta as required to operate the WhatsApp Business Platform integration (message delivery, template submission, phone number registration). We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data retention & deletion

Account and message data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time via support. We also honor Meta's data deletion callback requirements for information originating from the WhatsApp Business Platform.

6. Security

WhatsApp access tokens are stored encrypted at rest. Access to tenant data is scoped per account. See our platform documentation for further technical detail.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the in-app Support page, or to the contact address listed on our homepage.
