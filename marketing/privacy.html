Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026. This policy applies to the Wasi website, the Wasi web app at wasi.sirahagents.com, and the Wasi Android app.

1. Who we are

Wasi is a WhatsApp Business CRM operated by Sirah Digital, India ("Wasi", "we", "us"). We are a Meta Tech Provider: businesses ("clients") use Wasi to send and receive WhatsApp messages with their own customers through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).

We play two roles. For the account details of the people who use Wasi (our clients and their team members), we decide how that data is used. For the contacts, chats and messages a client manages in Wasi, we process that data only on the client's instructions, to provide the service to them. The client is responsible for having a lawful basis, including any required consent, to message their own customers.

2. Information we collect

Account information: business name, contact person's name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash). Team members invited by a client: name, email and role.

WhatsApp Business Account information: WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, display phone number, and the access token Meta issues when you connect your account. The token is stored encrypted.

Customer data you manage in Wasi: your contacts (names, phone numbers, tags and any custom fields you add), chat conversations and message content, media you send or receive, message templates, campaigns, automation flows, and opt-in/opt-out records.

Billing information: plan, invoices and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's payment page and handled by Razorpay; we do not receive or store them.

Technical and usage information: server logs (such as IP address, request time and errors), login times, and records of actions taken in your account, kept for security, troubleshooting and audit.

Support: anything you send us when you ask for help.

3. The Android app

The Wasi Android app displays the same Wasi web app inside the phone. It requests only the Internet permission. It does not read your phone's contacts, call logs, SMS, location, camera or microphone. Files are uploaded only when you choose them yourself. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS only).

4. How we use information

We use information to: provide the service (deliver and receive WhatsApp messages, run your campaigns and automations, show your inbox); connect and maintain your WhatsApp Business Account; sign you in and keep your account secure; send service emails such as verification, password reset and billing notices; send billing notices to you over WhatsApp; process payments; provide support; and meet our obligations under Meta's platform terms and applicable law.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your customers' messages for any purpose other than providing the service to you.

5. Who we share information with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run Wasi, and only for that purpose:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform): to send and receive messages, submit templates and register phone numbers. Meta's own terms and privacy policy also apply to WhatsApp messages.
  • Supabase: database hosting.
  • Render: application hosting.
  • Resend: delivery of service emails.
  • Razorpay: payment processing.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

Wasi does not currently send message content to any artificial-intelligence model provider. If we introduce such a feature, it will be off unless you turn it on, and we will update this policy before it launches.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data and the customer data you manage for as long as your account is active. When an account is deleted, its data is removed from our live systems, except records we must keep by law (for example invoices and payment records under Indian tax law) and limited security and audit logs.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Wasi account and its associated data at any time. See how to delete your account. We also act on Meta's data deletion requests for information that came from the WhatsApp Business Platform.

If you are a customer of a business that uses Wasi and want your data removed, contact that business first, as they control your data. You can also contact us and we will pass your request to them. Replying STOP to a business on WhatsApp opts you out of its marketing messages.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted at rest and passwords are stored as hashes. Each client's data is isolated from other clients' data at the database level. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance with us. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

Wasi is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also notify account holders by email.

12. Contact and grievances
