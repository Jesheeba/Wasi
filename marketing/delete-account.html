Delete your Wasi account

How to request deletion of a Wasi account and its data. This applies to the Wasi web app and the Wasi Android app (in.sirahdigital.wasi), which share the same account.

1. Send a deletion request

Email support@sirahdigital.in with the subject Delete my Wasi account, from the email address registered on the account. Include your business name. Only the account owner can request deletion of the whole account.

2. We confirm and delete

We reply to confirm the request came from the account owner, then delete the account within 30 days of that confirmation. Your WhatsApp Business Account itself stays with you on Meta; we only disconnect Wasi from it.

3. What is deleted

Your account and login details, team members, connected WhatsApp account details and access token, contacts, chats and messages, media, templates, campaigns, automation flows, API keys and settings.

4. What we keep, and for how long

Invoices and payment records, for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 8 years). Limited security and audit logs, which are not used for any other purpose. Messages you already sent through WhatsApp remain on your customers' phones and are outside our control.

Deleting only some data

You do not need to delete your account to remove data. You can delete contacts, templates and flows yourself inside the app, or email us to ask for specific data to be removed.

Customers of a business that uses Wasi

If a business messaged you through Wasi and you want your data removed, contact that business; they control it. You can also email us and we will pass your request on. See our Privacy Policy.
