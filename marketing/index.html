Know what you're signing up for

WhatsApp Cloud API vs. the WhatsApp Business App

These are two different products from Meta — knowing the difference explains why Wasi (and every real CRM) needs the Embedded Signup step during onboarding.

What Wasi uses

WhatsApp Cloud API

Meta's official, cloud-hosted business API. A CRM like Wasi connects to your number through a verified Meta app, so multiple people can work from one shared inbox with automation, templates and analytics.

  • Multiple team members, one shared inbox
  • Automation, broadcast campaigns, template messages
  • Requires a Meta Business Manager + WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)
  • Per-conversation pricing billed by Meta, passed through at cost
Not this

WhatsApp Business App

The free mobile app for small businesses. It's a single-device, single-user experience — great for a solo shopkeeper, but it can't power a CRM, automation, or a multi-agent inbox.

  • One phone, one person logged in at a time
  • No API access — can't connect to a CRM
  • No automation, campaigns, or reporting
  • Free, but doesn't scale past one person

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to connect WhatsApp?

A Facebook Business Manager account and a phone number that isn't already active on the regular WhatsApp Business App. During setup, Meta may ask you to verify your business (name, address, website) before your account can send templated messages at scale — this is Meta's requirement, not ours, and typically takes anywhere from a few minutes to a couple of days.

Who pays for the WhatsApp messages themselves?

Meta charges per-conversation fees directly, separate from your Wasi subscription. Your Wasi plan pays for the CRM software (inbox, automation, campaigns, analytics); Meta's conversation pricing passes through at cost based on your usage.

Who owns my contacts and conversation data?

You do. Your contacts, chat history and templates belong to your business and are scoped to your account only. You can export your data or close your account at any time.

How is this different from a manual BSP setup?

Traditional WhatsApp Business Solution Providers often require you to email business documents and wait days for a human to configure your number. Wasi uses Meta's official Embedded Signup flow to do that configuration automatically the moment you connect — no back-and-forth required.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from account settings; changes apply from your next billing cycle.
